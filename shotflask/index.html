FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

We Know What You're Thinking

Isn't this just a very expensive thimble?

No. A thimble is not double-walled. A thimble is not vacuum-insulated. A thimble does not have a precision-machined lid with a medical-grade silicone seal. Please don't insult us.

Why would I need my espresso to stay hot for 4+ hours?

You wouldn't. But knowing it could is the entire point. It's like owning a sports car in a city with a 30mph speed limit. The capability is the luxury.

Can I put other liquids in it?

Technically yes. Spiritually no. The ShotFlask™ was designed for espresso and espresso alone. Putting water in it would be like using a Stradivarius as a doorstop.

Why is it so expensive?

You're not paying for the material. You're paying for what we removed. It takes extraordinary engineering to make something this small work this well. Also, branding.

Do you offer a larger size?

Absolutely not. If you want more coffee, buy two ShotFlasks. Or three. We call that "The Italian Breakfast Bundle" and yes, it's $299.99.

Is this a joke?

We take micro-hydration very seriously. Next question.
